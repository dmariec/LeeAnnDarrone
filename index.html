About Lee Ann

Faith-centered care for the whole person.

As a Christian counselor, I care for the whole person, mind, body, and spirit, blending professional counseling with hope and compassion. My work is guided by biblical principles and rooted in grace, and I'll meet you right where you are, without judgment.

Wherever you are in your story, I am committed to walking alongside you on your journey toward healing and wholeness.

In our work together, you set the direction. We build practical, evidence-based skills you can carry long after a session ends, drawing on Cognitive Behavioral Therapy and other proven approaches chosen to fit you, so you can heal from the past while facing what's in front of you today.

My Approach

An integrative, whole-person approach

No single method fits everyone, so I blend the approaches below to match where you are and what you need, whether that's easing anxiety, working through grief, or healing from trauma.

🧠

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy

Practical tools to shift the thought patterns that fuel anxiety and depression.

🎯

Solution Focused Therapy

Goal-oriented sessions that build on your strengths to create change you can see.

👪

Family Systems Therapy

Understanding how relationships and family dynamics shape our wellbeing.

🌿

Mindfulness

Grounding, present-moment skills to steady you through stress.

🧘

Trauma-Focused Therapy

Gentle, evidence-based care for healing from past wounds.

👀

Brainspotting

A focused method for processing and releasing stored trauma.

Your First Session

What to expect when we begin

During our first session, we'll explore what brought you to therapy and get to know each other to see if I would be a good fit for you. Counseling is much more effective when we have built a good therapeutic relationship.

I primarily use an eclectic approach. Together, we'll look at how your thoughts affect your emotions, behavior, and experiences. With therapy, you can learn positive ways to change those thoughts.
